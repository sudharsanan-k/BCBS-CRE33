CRE33.1 – CRE33.2 • Supervisory Slotting Framework

IRB Supervisory Slotting Approach for Specialised Lending

Specialised lending funds a single-purpose asset or project. Cash flows generated by that specific asset repay the debt. Many banks lack enough historical default data to estimate Probability of Default (PD) under the corporate IRB model. Those banks must apply CRE33. Under CRE33, the bank scores deal attributes and maps its internal grade to one of five supervisory slots. Each slot assigns fixed risk weights for Unexpected Loss (UL) and Expected Loss (EL).

Banking Example: A bank lends $100.00M to build a municipal water plant. The bank cannot validate an internal corporate PD model for infrastructure loans. Analysts evaluate the deal under Table CRE33.13 and assign the Strong supervisory slot.
Benchmark Calculation ($100M PF) Slot: Strong (Std)
1. Exposure at Default (EAD): $100.00M
2. UL Risk Weight (CRE33.2): 70% → $70.00M RWA
3. Min UL Capital (8% × RWA): $5.60M
4. EL Risk Weight (CRE33.9): 5%
5. Expected Loss (8% × 5% × $100M): $0.40M

Step-by-Step Regulatory Formula Breakdown

CRE33.2 – CRE33.10
1. Credit RWA: Multiply EAD by the UL risk weight. $100.00M × 50% = $50.00M
2. Minimum UL Capital: Multiply RWA by 8%. 8% × $50.00M = $4.00M
3. Expected Loss (EL): Multiply 8% by EL risk weight and EAD. 8% × 0% × $100.00M = $0.00M
4. CRE35 Provision Check: Compare provisions to EL and 0.6% RWA cap. Excess $0.50M | Cap $0.30M
